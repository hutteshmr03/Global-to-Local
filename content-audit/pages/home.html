Made by hand, start to finish

Four steps from her kitchen to your shelf

80% of what we stock is made by women from self-help groups across Goa. Nothing here comes off a line.

  1. 01

    It starts in her kitchen

    A recipe from her own house — a masala, a pickle, a batch of papads — ground and mixed by hand, in quantities a kitchen can actually manage.

  2. 02

    Dried in the sun, cured slowly

    Kokum sola on cotton, papads on the roof, sola in the shade. The coast does half the work and it will not be hurried.

  3. 03

    Onto the shelf in Fontainhas

    It reaches Tarcar Mansion and goes out on the shelf beside the rest — one shop, thirteen shelves, all of it from the state.

  4. 04

    Packed in cane, sent on

    Wrapped and set into a cane basket by hand. Ask at the store or write to us — hampers are put together in Goa.
